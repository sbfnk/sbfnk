I'm Professor of Infectious Disease Dynamics at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine, where I co-direct the [Centre for Mathematical Modelling of Infectious Diseases](https://cmmid.github.io) and lead the [epiforecasts](https://epiforecasts.io) group.

My work aims to make the best possible use of data to understand and control infectious diseases. I develop statistical methods for estimating transmission dynamics from sparse and uncertain data, and open-source software that puts them to use.

### Software

Developed with the epiforecasts group and collaborators:

- [EpiNow2](https://epiforecasts.io/EpiNow2/): real-time reproduction numbers, nowcasts and forecasts
- [scoringutils](https://epiforecasts.io/scoringutils/): evaluating probabilistic forecasts
- [socialmixr](https://github.com/epiforecasts/socialmixr): contact matrices from social mixing surveys
- [EpiBranch.jl](https://github.com/epiforecasts/EpiBranch.jl): composable branching-process models of outbreaks
- [rbi](https://github.com/epiforecasts/rbi): Bayesian inference for state-space models via LibBi
